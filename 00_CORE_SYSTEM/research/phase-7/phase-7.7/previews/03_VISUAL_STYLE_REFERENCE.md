## 1. Aesthetic Direction: Contemporary Indie Animation

The artistic style of *"पहला ऑर्डर"* (The First Order) draws inspiration from celebrated modern 2D animated works (such as *Klaus*, Studio Mir, and contemporary Kyoto Animation slice-of-life productions). It emphasizes:
- **Warm, tactile natural textures**: Rustic polished walnut wood counters, brushed stainless steel, sheer fluttering linen curtains, textured paper boxes, and warm clay pottery.
- **Atmospheric Golden Hour Lighting**: Volumetric sunbeams streaming through an industrial arched studio window, illuminating floating flour dust motes and creating crisp rim-highlights on character silhouettes.
- **Intentional Depth & Staging**: Strict three-plane spatial separation (Foreground props $\rightarrow$ Middle-ground character acting zone $\rightarrow$ Background sunlit window and city horizon).
