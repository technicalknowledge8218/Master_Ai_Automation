## 2. Detailed Panel Specifications

### Panel 1: Scene 1 — The Weight of Silence (`00:00 – 00:08`)
- **Shot Framing**: Medium-wide eye-level shot across the sunlit kitchen counter.
- **Composition**: Aanya staged in the left third, slumped over the counter; Kabir in the right third on a high stool sipping chai. Between them sits the handcrafted cupcake gift box and the silent smartphone.
- **Camera Movement**: Subtle slow push-in ($1.00\times \rightarrow 1.06\times$), drawing the viewer into Aanya's internal emotional struggle.
- **Lighting**: Cool 4500K morning diffuse light reflecting off grey tiles, emphasizing loneliness and self-doubt.
- **Dialogue (Aanya)**: *"तीन हफ्ते हो गए कबीर... एक भी कस्टमर नहीं आया। शायद मुझे ये सब बंद कर देना चाहिए।"*
- **SFX**: Gentle ceramic cutting-chai glass clinking onto counter at $4.5\text{s}$.

---

### Panel 2: Scene 2 — The Electric Ding (`00:08 – 00:16`)
- **Shot Framing**: Dynamic medium shot focusing on the space between brother and sister.
- **Camera Movement**: Snappy whip-pan focus down to the phone screen.
- **Lighting**: Sudden dramatic shift—a 6500K bright electric cyan bloom bursts from the phone screen across both characters' faces and hands.
- **Acting & Animation**:
  - Kabir leans forward with an encouraging smile.
  - At $10.2\text{s}$, the notification rings: Kabir's eyebrows shoot up, Aanya gasps, pupils dilate, and Kabir points directly to the screen.
- **Dialogue (Kabir)**: *"दीदी, इतनी जल्दी हिम्मत हारोगी?... अरे सुनो! फ़ोन पर क्या बजा?"*
- **SFX**: High-fidelity modern digital order chime: **'DING!'** ($10.2\text{s}$).

---

### Panel 3: Scene 3 — The First Order Unlocked (`00:16 – 00:23`)
- **Shot Framing**: Emotive close-up, split between the glowing phone screen and Aanya's face.
- **Visual Staging**:
  - Phone screen displays verified order banner: *"ORDER #1001: 24 Chocolate Truffle Cupcakes — Confirmed!"*
  - Aanya's hands tremble as she holds the phone.
  - A single glistening tear droplet rolls down her cheek.
  - Her trembling mouth opens into a radiant, tearful smile.
- **Camera Movement**: Handheld subtle camera breathing motion ($0.03\text{ rad}$ jitter) reflecting human emotional shock.
- **Lighting**: Warm 3500K golden amber key-light filling the frame, washing away the cold tones of Scene 1.
- **Dialogue (Aanya)**: *"कबीर... देखो! सच में पहला ऑर्डर आ गया!"*
- **SFX**: Fabric rustle as Kabir pulls out and unfolds the baker's apron ($16.8\text{s}$).

---

### Panel 4: Scene 4 — The Baker's Apron & The Dawn (`00:23 – 00:30`)
- **Shot Framing**: Wide cinematic hero shot.
- **Visual Staging**:
  - Kabir grins proudly, giving an enthusiastic thumbs-up.
  - Aanya stands tall in center frame, wrapping the navy-blue canvas apron around her waist.
  - Her hands firmly pull the apron straps and tie a clean knot at her back.
  - She looks up toward the studio window with a confident, radiant smile.
  - Warm golden morning sunbeams stream through the sheer curtains, illuminating floating flour dust particles.
- **Camera Movement**: Wide triumphant pull-back ($1.08\times \rightarrow 1.00\times$) revealing the entire bright kitchen studio.
- **Lighting**: Soaring 3200K golden hour sunlight flooding the kitchen in warm yellow and amber.
- **Dialogue (Kabir)**: *"कहा था ना मैंने! अब अपना एप्रन पहनिए शेफ़ साहिबा... आपकी नई शुरुआत हो गई!"*
- **SFX**: Crisp apron tie snap ($24.0\text{s}$) & distant morning bird chirp outside the window ($26.5\text{s}$).
