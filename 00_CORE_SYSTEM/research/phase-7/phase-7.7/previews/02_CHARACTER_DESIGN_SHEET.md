## 1. Design Philosophy & Anti-Cliché Rules

In strict adherence to the Phase 7.7 mandate:
- **Zero primitive shapes**: No circles/rectangles as heads or torsos; no stick figures; no emoji faces; no PowerPoint clip-art.
- **True Anatomical Proportions**: Stylized modern 2D animation proportions with realistic skeletal structure, joint placement, facial planes, and expressive eyes.
- **Consistent Visual Model**: Exact RGB hex swatches, silhouette outlines, and costume blueprints enforce 100% visual consistency across all scenes.

## 2. Character Model 1: Aanya (आन्या) — Protagonist

### 2.1 Anatomical & Physical Profile
- **Age**: 22 years old.
- **Height / Proportions**: 5'4" (162 cm), **7.5 heads** tall. Slender athletic build with realistic shoulder span and articulated collarbone.
- **Head & Facial Structure**:
  - Soft oval jawline tapering to a gentle chin.
  - Almond-shaped hazel-amber eyes (`#78350F`) with distinct pupils, catchlights, and natural eyelashes.
  - Expressive dark brown eyebrows (`#3D2314`) capable of subtle micro-expressions (anxious slant, surprised arch, smiling teardrop squint).
  - Defined nose bridge with warm shadow contour (`#C8784D`).
  - Natural rose-petal lip contours (`#BE123C`).
- **Hairstyle**:
  - Dark coffee-brown hair (`#3D2314` base, `#1F1108` shadow).
  - Styled into a contemporary textured top-knot bun with loose, face-framing curls and wisps along the temples.

### 2.2 Wardrobe & Props
- **Top**: Oversized cream ribbed-linen sweater (`#FDFBF7`) with sleeves casually rolled up to mid-forearm.
- **Bottom**: High-waisted charcoal denim trousers (`#334155`).
- **Footwear**: Clean white retro canvas sneakers (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Key Prop (Hero Item)**: Custom navy-blue canvas baker's apron (`#1E293B`) with adjustable tan leather straps (`#B45309`), solid brass buckles (`#F59E0B`), and an embroidered whisk emblem on the front chest pocket.

### 2.3 Key Expression Studies (Illustrated in Model Sheet)
1. **Anxious Doubt (Scene 1)**: Slumped inner brows, troubled hazel eyes, nervous lower-lip bite.
2. **Sudden Gasp (Scene 2)**: Arched brows, dilated pupils, wide O-shaped mouth upon hearing the notification chime.
3. **Tearful Relief (Scene 3)**: Soft crescent smiling eyes with glistening blue tear droplets (`#38BDF8`), open joyful smile showing upper teeth.
4. **Resolute Baker (Scene 4)**: Confident focused gaze, firm chin, radiant triumphant smile as she ties her apron strings.
