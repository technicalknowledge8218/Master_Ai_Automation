## 7. REMAINING LIMITATIONS & PHASE 8 ROADMAP

1. **Character Lip Sync**: While facial expressions (blinking, smiling, singing mouth shapes) are animated, exact per-phoneme viseme lip sync (rhubarb / wav2lip) would further elevate close-up vocal realism.
2. **Dynamic Skeletal Rigging**: Current 2D characters use forward-kinematics and procedural geometric deformation. Integrating an open-source 2D skeletal bone rig (e.g. Spine or DragonBones format) will enable complex running, jumping, and nuanced hand gesture animation.
3. **Soundtrack Richness**: The procedural acoustic score demonstrates multi-instrument arrangement, but integrating licensed royalty-free instrumental recordings from Creative Commons repositories will provide acoustic richness for future commercial releases.
