### 1. Curriculum Overview

The Video Director Academy curriculum consists of 12 intensive specialist tracks designed to transform Master AI into a complete, self-aware creative production studio. Each module defines:
- **Core Directorial Objectives**: What the AI must master.
- **Practical Exercises**: Short, executable tests designed to prove competency in isolation.
- **Reference Film Grammar & Examples**: Cinematic rules derived from world cinema and animation masterpieces.
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Specific quantitative and qualitative benchmarks.
- **Historical Weaknesses**: Exactly what went wrong in previous phases.
- **Reusable Production Improvements**: Concrete code, algorithms, and agent behaviors implemented.

### 4. Integration with Practical Exercises

The Academy mandates that before producing complete multi-minute productions, the training loop must successfully execute and evaluate **7 practical training exercises**:
- **Exercise 1 (Cinematography)**: 5s Eased Zoom with optical depth (`ex01_cinematic_zoom.mp4`).
- **Exercise 2 (Character Acting)**: 5s Involuntary reaction and eye saccade (`ex02_character_reaction.mp4`).
- **Exercise 3 (Dialogue & Lip-Sync)**: 5s Speech articulation to natural Hindi audio (`ex03_dialogue_lipsync.mp4`).
- **Exercise 4 (Suspense Editing)**: 5s Metric cutting and countdown tension (`ex04_suspense_editing.mp4`).
- **Exercise 5 (Action & Impact)**: 5s Vehicle drift and damped impact shake (`ex05_action_impact.mp4`).
- **Exercise 6 (Continuity Multi-Shot)**: 10s 3-shot sequence with 180-degree rule continuity (`ex06_continuity_multishot.mp4`).
- **Exercise 7 (Comedy Timing)**: 10s Deadpan pause and visual punchline (`ex07_comedy_timing.mp4`).
