## 1. Narrative Concept & Dramatic Premise

- **Title**: *"आखिरी चिट्ठी"* (*The Last Letter*)
- **Genre**: Emotional Family Drama / Mystery & Suspense / Psychological Realism
- **Target Runtime**: 59.00s Net Master Video (67.0s Gross across 9 scenes with 8x 1.0s crossfades)
- **Primary Language**: Hindi (Khari Boli / Literary Hindustani)
- **Core Theme**: Hidden maternal sacrifice, filial guilt, the burden of unspoken love, and the confrontation with mortality.

### The Dramatic Twist
A young software engineer, **Rohit**, returns to his decaying ancestral home ten years after his mother **Sharda Devi** passed away. For years, he lived under the belief that his mother had successfully financed his prestigious foreign engineering education through wise ancestral investments. While cleaning an antique writing desk in her abandoned room, he discovers a hidden brass keepsake box containing a yellowed, unmailed letter written in her fading handwriting.

The letter reveals the devastating truth: she was diagnosed with Stage-3 malignant cancer during his second year of university. Rather than spending their limited family savings on expensive chemotherapy and hospital stays, she deliberately stopped her medical treatments and quietly endured the fatal progression of her illness, channeling every single rupee into Rohit's tuition fees. His celebrated graduation degree was funded by her life.
