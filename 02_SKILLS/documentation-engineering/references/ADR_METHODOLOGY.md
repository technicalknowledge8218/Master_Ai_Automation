# Architecture Decision Record (ADR) Methodology

> **Reference**: `02_SKILLS/documentation-engineering/references/`  

---

## 1. What is an ADR?
An **Architecture Decision Record (ADR)** captures a single architectural decision along with its context and consequences. ADRs are immutable historical logs of why software evolved in a specific direction.

## 2. Standard ADR Format
1. **Title**: Sequential identifier and title (e.g., `ADR-001: Adopt Three-Tier Decoupled Architecture`).
2. **Status**: `PROPOSED`, `ACCEPTED`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `DEPRECATED`.
3. **Context**: What is the problem, business need, or technical limitation?
4. **Decision**: What choice was made, and what alternative solutions were rejected?
5. **Consequences**: What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what technical debt is incurred?
